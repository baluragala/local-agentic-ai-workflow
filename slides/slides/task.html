01 · BUSINESS PROBLEM

ShopEase Returns Desk

INPUT · ONE CUSTOMER MESSAGE

“Hi, my headphones from order ORD-1002 arrived with a cracked headband. I’d like to return them.”

Policy: electronics 15 days, apparel and home 30 days, gift cards non-returnable. A 10% restocking fee applies when the customer simply changed their mind.

Expected outcome: exactly one of

ticket_created + correct refund

declined + the policy reason

answered (order status)

order_not_found

needs_info → ask the customer

out_of_scope

Define the task before any code: input, goal, expected outcomes, rules. The outcome list on the right becomes the status field of our state, and later the thing we evaluate. Emphasise that outcomes are business outcomes, not text quality.
