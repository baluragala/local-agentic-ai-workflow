05 · WHAT ARE WE EVALUATING?

Three levels that can disagree

LEVEL 1

Model response quality

Is the reply fluent, polite, accurate? Human review, LLM-as-judge, heuristics.

LEVEL 2

Workflow execution

Allowed actions, right order, valid calls? Criteria 1–5.

LEVEL 3

Business outcome

Was the task actually done correctly? Criterion 7: ticket + correct refund.

✗

The lying model scores yes on fluency and fails step completion, final output and business outcome. Fluency is not success.
