02 · MODEL CHOICE

qwen2.5:1.5b is small, but enough to show the mechanics

Why it is sufficient

  • ~1 GB, runs on free Colab CPU/T4 or a laptop
  • Instruction-tuned with native tool calling
  • Works with JSON-schema structured output
  • Swap to qwen2.5:3b or llama3.2:3b by changing one line
BenefitsLimitations
Free and privateWeaker reasoning, stops early
No keys, no rate limitsHallucinates tools, args, numbers
Works offlineSensitive to prompt wording
Ideal for learningNot a benchmark of large models

The limitations are why the orchestrator validates, enforces rules and checks completion. Guardrails matter more with small models.

Be explicit that we are not benchmarking the model. Its mistakes give us real material for debugging and evaluation in Sections 4 and 5.
